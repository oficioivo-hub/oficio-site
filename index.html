How it works

  1. You describe the job and send one sample file or one URL. No call needed.
  2. I reply with a written scope: what you get, what I won't do, price and delivery date.
  3. You approve. I start. Half upfront on jobs over USD 100, the rest on delivery.
  4. You check the result against the scope. If it doesn't match, you don't pay.

I don't take work that requires meetings, phone calls or on-site presence. Everything is written, which is why it's fast and why there's a paper trail.
