How it works

  1. You send the job. One sample file or one URL. No call needed.
  2. I reply with a written scope. What you get, what I won't do, the price and the delivery date.
  3. You approve. I start. Half upfront on jobs over USD 100.
  4. You check it against the scope. If it doesn't match, you don't pay.

06

Questions

Is this AI-generated?

Partly, and I'd rather say it now. I use automation for the first pass and review every deliverable before it ships. You test the output against the written scope before paying, so what matters is whether it works, not what wrote it. If you need a human to type every character, I'm the wrong fit.

What if it doesn't match the scope?

You don't pay. The scope is written before I start, so "matching" isn't a matter of opinion — it's a checklist both of us already have.

Do you sign an NDA?

Yes. Send yours, or use a standard mutual one. Signing takes a day, not a week.

What happens to my data?

I work on a copy, deliver the result and delete the copy within seven days. I don't keep client data, don't resell it and don't use it to train anything.

Can you start today?

No. 24 hours minimum, 48 for most jobs. That gap is the review, and it's the reason the work arrives finished.
