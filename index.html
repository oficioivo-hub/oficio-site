How it works

  1. You send the job. One sample file or one URL. No call needed.
  2. I reply with a written scope. What you get, what I won't do, price and delivery date.
  3. You approve. I start. Half upfront over USD 100.
  4. You check it against the scope. If it doesn't match, you don't pay.

06

Questions

Is this AI-generated?

Partly, and I'd rather say it now. I use automation for the first pass and review every deliverable before it ships. You test the output against the written scope before paying, so what matters is whether it works. If you need a human to type every character, I'm the wrong fit.

What if it doesn't match the scope?

You don't pay. The scope is written before I start, so "matching" is a checklist we both already have.

Do you sign an NDA?

Yes. Send yours, or use a standard mutual one.

What happens to my data?

I work on a copy, deliver the result and delete the copy within seven days. No client data is kept, resold or used to train anything.

Can you start today?

No. 24 hours minimum, 48 for most jobs. That gap is the review, and it's why the work arrives finished.
