Who you are dealing with

Quotes and scope

Every job starts with a written scope: what you receive, what is excluded, the acceptance criteria, the price and the delivery date. The price in that quote is the price you pay. Work does not start until you approve it in writing.

Payment

  • Jobs over USD 100: half upfront, half on delivery.
  • Jobs under USD 100: paid on delivery.
  • Payment by bank transfer or card against an invoice issued from Chile.

Acceptance and refunds

You check the delivery against the acceptance criteria in the scope. If it does not match, you do not pay, and any amount already paid for that job is returned. If it does match, the job is complete. Changes outside the original scope are a new quote.

Revisions

Fixes for defects in what was delivered are free and done the same day. Changes of mind are not defects and are quoted separately.

Your data

Files you send are used only to do the job, are not shared, and are deleted within seven days of delivery. See the privacy policy.

Ownership

On full payment, you own the deliverable, including the scripts and the documentation. The workshop keeps no licence over it. Public demos on this site are separate work and are not derived from client data.

What the workshop does not do

No calls, no on-site work, no rush jobs under 24 hours, and no work that requires hiding how it is produced. If a job needs any of those, it is declined in writing.

Liability

The workshop's total liability for any job is limited to the amount paid for that job. It is not liable for indirect losses, lost profit or decisions you take based on the deliverable.

Governing terms

These terms apply to work agreed from 17 November 2026. A signed quote with different terms takes precedence over this page for that job.
