Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Oficio is a small technical workshop operated by Ivo Ivonovich Fernández. Contact: oficio.ivo@gmail.com.

What this site collects

Nothing. The site is static, has no cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels and no third-party scripts. There is no form and no account system.

What the workshop collects when you contact it

  • The email address you write from, and what you write in it.
  • The files or links you choose to send as a sample of the job.

Those are used to answer you and to do the job. They are not sold, not shared and not used to train any model. Job files are deleted within seven days of delivery.

The X account (@oficioivo)

The workshop publishes its own posts through the X API. The application stores only the text, identifier and URL of its own published posts, in a local business ledger. It does not collect third-party content, does not send automated direct messages, does not automate follows, likes or reposts, and does not use X data to train or evaluate models.

Your rights

Write to oficio.ivo@gmail.com to ask what is held about you, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted. Requests are answered within seven days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. This page is the only version that applies.
